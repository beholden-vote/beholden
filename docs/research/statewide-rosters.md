# Statewide rosters of county and city officials: 50 states plus DC

Research date: 2026-10-03. Question: for each state, is there a government or public-university roster that covers EVERY county or EVERY city (the officials themselves, not just a directory of governments)? Quotes are verbatim from pages as fetched (curly quotes may show as replacement characters in my fetch). Verdicts record what the published wording supports; where terms are silent or unread the verdict is UNDETERMINED, and no licence question was decided by inference.

## Method and honest limits

- Discovery: one web search per state for a state-agency or public-university roster, then one fetch of the best candidate and (where reachable) its terms or disclaimer page, via Python urllib. No crawling, no downloads of data files, no forms submitted.
- Many state sites answered HTTP 403 or a bot challenge to my fetch (Ohio SoS roster, Massachusetts SoS, Mississippi SoS, Arkansas portal, NH Dept of Transportation-hosted PDF, New York DOS, Wisconsin Elections, Arizona SoS) or HTTP 500 (Illinois Comptroller) or did not load (Pennsylvania munstats). I did not work around any of them. They are marked "not read".
- "Roster" column legend: **EVERY** = the source claims to cover all counties and/or all cities/towns with their officials (checked on at least one page unless marked "per search"); **PARTIAL** = only some offices (e.g. clerks) or only counties or only cities; **NONE** = no government or public-university roster found (associations such as leagues of cities and county associations exist in most states but are private non-profits, not government, so they are noted but do not count).
- "Terms found" is wording from the roster page or the site-wide terms page I could read. A site-wide state-portal disclaimer is not necessarily the governing terms for that dataset.
- Verdict format: **Free / Paid** = may facts be republished on a free public site with attribution / included in a sold compiled dataset. Tennessee: see the existing research in `docs/research/local-sources-2026-10.md` sections 2 and 3 (CTAS and MTAS, awaiting written permission); not repeated here.
- Personal data: these rosters carry officials' emails and sometimes home-style addresses; keep to official public roles and official contact channels.

## Best next eight states (shortlist)

Ranked on: (a) a government or public-university owner, (b) coverage of every county or city including governing-body members, (c) machine-readable access without a form or CAPTCHA, (d) no hostile wording found, (e) freshness. None is cleared; every one needs the usual written confirmation of reuse before it ships.

1. **Kentucky (DLG).** Verified: every county and city, council members with title and email, per-record HTML, current. Terms: no licence but no prohibition; the Privacy page warns "State law restricts the use of personally identifiable information for commercial purposes", so keep to official contact channels and ask DLG. Scrapable but HTML-only.
2. **Alaska (DCRA Community Database).** Verified: machine-readable ArcGIS Hub feed (JSON) covering every borough, city and community, plus an Entity Contacts Directory; a disclaimer that expressly frames the data as useful "for research purposes". Small state, easy first pilot; officials' per-entity fields still to confirm.
3. **Maryland (State Archives Manual).** Verified index: all counties and 157 municipalities with office pages; terms ask for citation and credit and put copyright assessment on the user. Low volume, stable HTML; get the citation wording agreed.
4. **Pennsylvania (DCED Municipal Statistics and PennDOT layer).** Large unit count (2,572 municipalities in the PennDOT layer, which is structured ArcGIS data); officials are self-reported annually by every county and municipality. DCED portal was not reachable, so verify coverage of governing-body members.
5. **Connecticut (Register and Manual, Section VII).** Verified: every town, city and borough with officers supplied by the clerks; disclaimer silent on data; update cadence is periodic, so check dates.
6. **Illinois (Comptroller local-government contacts).** Largest local-unit count (about 1,400 cities and villages plus counties and townships) with self-reported contacts; not read (HTTP 500), so confirm structure and terms first.
7. **Ohio (Secretary of State Roster).** Official roster of municipal and township officials; not read (WAF 403), so verify structure and terms.
8. **Vermont (Secretary of State).** Every town's clerk, treasurer and selectboard in PDF listings (per search); URL moved, so locate the current page. Small and stable.

Considered and left out: Delaware (a good public-university roster, but its legal notice says "Redistribution or commercial use is prohibited without express written permission"); Michigan (commercial use and resale prohibited by the state's site terms, roster scope unverified); Colorado (a complete roster, but behind a checkbox plus reCAPTCHA and described as "intended for interactive use by individuals", so only worth pursuing by asking DOLA for a data release).
